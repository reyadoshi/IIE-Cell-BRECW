04 / FROM SPARK TO STARTUP

Your idea deserves
a next step.

You don’t need a finished business plan to begin. Start with a problem you care about, then build evidence one step at a time.

  1. 01

    Spot & submit

    Identify a real need. Capture the problem, who faces it and your first idea.

  2. 02

    Explore & screen

    Review relevance, feasibility and the value your idea could create.

  3. 03

    Meet a mentor

    Connect with guidance, technical expertise and the right resources.

  4. 04

    Build & test

    Create a prototype. Gather feedback from potential users and improve it.

  5. 05

    Pitch & incubate

    Shape a business model and explore a suitable incubation referral.

Progress is a cycle, not a straight line.Learn → Improve → Try again ↗

06 / THE OPERATING FRAMEWORK

Good ideas need
good foundations.

A proposed charter for a consistent, inclusive and accountable student club.

PROPOSED · SUBJECT TO COLLEGE APPROVAL
Membership & fair representation +

General membership is open to interested students across disciplines. Encourage representation across departments and years, with a clear registration process and participation expectations. Department representatives collect ideas and bring student feedback to the committee.

Selection, tenure & handover +

Publish eligibility and selection criteria before applications. Use a faculty-supervised application and interaction process. A one-year tenure is recommended, with a documented procedure for vacancies and inactive office-bearers. Complete a handover of records, resources and ongoing project actions.

Meetings & decisions +

Hold a monthly review and additional planning meetings as needed. Record agendas, minutes, decisions and action owners. The Faculty Coordinator should approve meeting quorum, voting procedures and delegated responsibilities before the charter is adopted.

Budgets, sponsorships & approvals +

Prepare a semester activity plan and budget. Maintain receipts, sponsorship records and expense summaries under faculty supervision. Obtain faculty approval before spending, making external commitments, publishing official updates or using the college name and logo externally.

Mentorship, resources & IP awareness +

Connect teams with appropriate mentors, laboratories and incubation facilities. Share relevant funding and competition opportunities. Handle unpublished ideas with care and obtain permission before public disclosure. Refer intellectual property ownership and protection questions to the college’s applicable policy and qualified guidance.

Documentation & monthly review +

The General Secretary maintains official club records; the Documentation Coordinator maintains activity evidence and project portfolios. Review active teams, prototypes, mentor sessions, user feedback, competition participation and incubation referrals alongside event attendance.

Conduct, inclusion & student concerns +

Promote respectful collaboration, equitable participation, honest reporting and appropriate credit for contributions. Students should raise club concerns through the Faculty Coordinator or the college’s established grievance channel. Follow applicable college policies for all activities.
